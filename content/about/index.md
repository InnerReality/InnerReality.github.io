I am a PhD holder in Civil Engineering who has recently graduated from University at Buffalo. A researcher by heart and a civil engineer by background, I value both rigor and pragmatism in research or a project that I undertake and I take pride in it. 

During my PhD, I have worked on rocking behavior of transformer bushing - from modelling of hysteresis of Belleville Washers[^BW], developing new way of modelling [rocking dynamics](https://innerreality.github.io/RockingDynamicsSite/) to shake table tests. I have also worked as a civil engineer for 4 years, involved in constructing bridges for some of the largest projects in India.

Beyond my experience, I am a curious thinker and tinkerer. For the past year, I have been working on a personal project involving computer networks, agentic workflows and applied machine learning. More importantly, I am fascinated by philosophy and application of complex systems from engineering to software development, finance and life in general.

If any of these resonates with you, feel free to connect.
